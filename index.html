Where paid traffic leaks.

Every booked job travels the same five steps. Most businesses have the ad and a page. Almost none have the free offer or the follow-up, and that gap is where we start.

  1. 1

    The ad

    Where it leaksThree promises at once, or none. People click out of curiosity and leave.

    What we doMeta ads with one clear promise, written, designed and run by us. Your Google ads tidied so each one opens the page that matches it.

  2. 2

    A free offer

    Usually missing

    Where it leaksMost people who click aren't ready to buy today. With nothing to take, they leave and don't come back.

    What we doSomething worth asking for now: a backyard design visit or a local price guide for pool builders, an open-dates and pricing check for venues.

  3. 3

    The page

    Where it leaksThe page doesn't repeat what the ad said, the reviews are hidden, and the form is screens away.

    What we doOne page per ad that says what the ad said, your Google rating on the first screen, and booking or a quote one tap away.

  4. 4

    The answer and follow-up

    Usually missing

    Where it leaksThe reply comes the next day, then stops. Buyers ask two or three businesses and book whoever answers first.

    What we doAn answer within a minute with prices and two times to choose from. Texts and emails keep going until they book or say no.

  5. 5

    The booked job

    Where it leaksNobody can say which ads turned into booked jobs, so budget keeps going to the ones that don't.

    What we doEvery lead tracked to a booked design visit, tour or job, and reported to you every Monday.

Every Monday, five numbers.

You see whether it's working without logging in anywhere: leads, booked jobs, cost per booked job, response time and revenue from booked jobs. Plus one change we made and the next thing we'll test.

Leads up but booked jobs flat? Then it's the phone, not the ads. We fix replies and follow-up before spending another dollar on ads.
