Questions owners ask.

What does Bookdzilla do?

Bookdzilla gets local businesses more booked customers from the leads they already get. We answer every new lead in under 60 seconds by text, email, web chat, Facebook or Instagram, follow up until they book, and put the appointment on your calendar. We also run Meta and Google ads when you need more leads coming in.

How do I get more patients for my dental clinic or med spa?

Most clinics lose new patients in the gap between the enquiry and the first reply. Answer every enquiry within a minute, give the price range they asked for, offer two appointment times, and send reminders. That alone books more of the patients you already pay to attract. Then add ads once the follow-up works.

What is speed to lead?

Speed to lead is the time between a new enquiry and your first real reply. The faster it is, the more of those leads book with you instead of a competitor. We bring it under 60 seconds, day and night, weekends included.

What is missed-call text back?

When you miss a call, the caller gets a text within seconds: sorry we missed you, how can we help, and a link or two times to book. Most callers who would have tried the next business reply to the text instead.

What is database reactivation?

It is a campaign to your old leads and past customers: people who asked for a quote and never booked, or patients who have not been in for a year. A short, personal text or email sequence turns part of that list into new bookings without spending on ads.

Do you work with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Calendly or Cal.com?

Yes. We build on GoHighLevel or HubSpot as the CRM, book into Calendly, Cal.com or your existing calendar, and connect the rest with Zapier, Make or n8n. If you already use one of these, we work inside it.

Is this an AI receptionist?

Part of it is. The assistant that answers your leads is built on Claude and ChatGPT, trained on your prices, services and policies, and it hands the conversation to you the moment someone asks for a person. You approve how it talks before anything goes live.

Do I need a new website?

No. Ads send people to a simple booking page we build, or straight into a text conversation. If your site works, we connect to it.

How soon does it start?

About two weeks from our first call to everything running, including your sign-off on the replies.

I already pay for leads from Angi, The Knot or Zocdoc.

Keep them. We answer those leads faster too. After 60 days, compare cost per booked customer side by side and keep whatever wins.

You do. The ad account, the CRM and every contact stay yours if you stop.
